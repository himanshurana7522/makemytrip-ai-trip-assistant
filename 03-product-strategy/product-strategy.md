
# MakeMyTrip AI Trip Assistant
## Product Strategy

### 1. Product Vision

To help travellers create personalised, budget-conscious trip plans while making AI-generated recommendations more transparent and trustworthy.

### 2. Product Objective

The product aims to reduce the effort involved in researching and planning trips across multiple platforms.

It focuses on helping travellers:
- Generate itineraries based on their travel preferences.
- Consider budget constraints while planning.
- Understand why particular recommendations are suggested.
- Identify information that requires independent confirmation.
- Save and revisit their trip plans.

### 3. Target Users

**Primary user: Aarav Mehta**

- 27-year-old working professional based in Bengaluru.
- Plans short trips and has limited time for travel research.
- Wants recommendations aligned with his interests and budget.
- Currently compares information across websites, maps, reviews and social platforms.

**Secondary user: Riya Sharma**

- 35-year-old working professional based in Delhi.
- Plans family trips involving travellers with different needs.
- Values convenience, comfort and suitable travel options.
- Needs practical recommendations and backup choices.

**Research note:** Aarav and Riya are proto-personas developed for the assignment. They have not been validated through documented user interviews.

### 4. Value Proposition

A personalised AI trip-planning assistant that considers traveller preferences and budget while distinguishing AI-generated suggestions from information that still needs confirmation.

**Core value pillars:**

1. Personalisation — recommendations based on traveller inputs.
2. Budget awareness — consideration of the traveller's stated budget.
3. Transparency — clear communication about AI-generated suggestions.
4. Trust — visible identification of information requiring confirmation.
5. Convenience — saving and revisiting generated itineraries.

### 5. Strategic Product Priorities

The original product research identified four opportunities:

| Opportunity | Strategic focus |
|---|---|
| Smart Itinerary Generator | Generate personalised travel plans |
| Conversational Trip Planner | Refine plans through conversation |
| Trust-Aware Itinerary Assistant | Make recommendations more transparent |
| Family-Safe Trip Planner | Address family travel requirements |

**Selected priority: Trust-Aware Itinerary Assistant**

The trust-aware concept was selected through the documented RICE prioritisation exercise.

Recorded RICE inputs:

- Reach: 10
- Impact: 5
- Confidence: 0.90
- Effort: 4
- RICE score: 11.25

The selection reflects the prioritisation assumptions in the original assignment, not validated market demand.

### 6. Product Scope

**Core product capabilities**

- Personalised itinerary generation.
- Destination, travel dates and duration inputs.
- Budget and traveller preference inputs.
- AI-generated travel recommendations.
- Saved trip management.
- Clear identification of information requiring confirmation.

**Out of scope for the documented prototype**

- Live flight or hotel booking.
- Payment processing.
- Guaranteed prices or availability.
- Autonomous changes to confirmed travel arrangements.
- Claims that unverified recommendations are confirmed facts.

### 7. Success Metrics

**North Star Metric**

24-hour Recommendation Action Rate

The percentage of users who save or start booking a recommended item within 24 hours of receiving an itinerary.

**Formula:**

Users who save or start booking a recommended item within 24 hours ÷ Users who received an itinerary × 100

**Guardrail Metric**

Budget Violation Rate

Tracks how frequently generated itineraries fail to respect the user's stated budget.

Other supporting metrics:
- Itinerary generation completion rate.
- Itinerary save rate.
- Recommendation interaction rate.
- User feedback on recommendation relevance.

**Measurement limitation:** The original assignment includes sample funnel calculations, but the available data does not establish a verified 24-hour conversion rate.

### 8. Product Principles

1. Never present unverified prices, availability, opening hours or ratings as confirmed facts.
2. Clearly distinguish user-provided information from AI-generated suggestions.
3. Mark uncertain information as requiring confirmation.
4. Communicate when the user's budget may be insufficient.
5. Never claim that a booking or payment has been completed without a real transaction.
6. Keep the traveller informed about the limitations of AI-generated recommendations.

### 9. Commercial Strategy — Initial Hypotheses

The original assignment proposed the following pricing structure:

| Plan | Proposed price |
|---|---:|
| Free | ₹0 |
| Trip Plus | ₹149/month |
| Trip Plus Family | ₹299/month |

These prices are initial product hypotheses. They have not been validated through willingness-to-pay research or actual customer purchases.

An illustrative revenue scenario in the assignment assumed:
- 10,000 monthly active users.
- 3% paid conversion.
- ₹149 monthly subscription.

This produces an illustrative gross monthly revenue of ₹44,700. It is a scenario calculation, not actual revenue or a validated forecast.

### 10. Current Product Status

The project is an independent educational AI product prototype developed using Google AI Studio as part of an AI Product Management assignment.

The documented work includes product research, proto-personas, competitive analysis, SWOT, opportunity prioritisation, PRD-lite, prototype development and AI evaluation.

The prototype and evaluation have documented limitations, including incomplete budget compliance and partially reliable automated logging.

It is not an official MakeMyTrip product and has not been demonstrated as a commercially launched product.
