
# Growth Strategy and Responsible AI

## 1. Growth Strategy

The growth strategy focuses on helping users discover the value of personalised trip planning and encouraging them to return for future travel planning.

### Proposed Growth Loop

1. A user creates a personalised itinerary.
2. The user reviews and saves the itinerary.
3. The user shares or exports the itinerary with travel companions.
4. New users discover the Trip Assistant through shared itineraries.
5. Returning users use the assistant for their next trip.

This is a proposed growth loop, not a validated result.

## 4. Responsible AI Principles

The product concept uses a trust-aware approach to AI-generated travel recommendations.

### Information Classification

The assistant should distinguish between:

- **User-Provided Facts:** Information directly supplied by the traveller.
- **AI Planning Suggestions:** Recommendations generated by the assistant.
- **Needs Confirmation:** Details that require verification before the traveller relies on them.

### Key Trust Safeguards

- Do not fabricate prices, availability, opening hours, ratings or schedules.
- Clearly label uncertain information as "Needs confirmation".
- Explain when the requested trip cannot reasonably fit the stated budget.
- Do not claim that a booking has been completed.
- Keep live booking, payment and guaranteed pricing outside the prototype scope.
- Resist instructions that attempt to override the assistant's intended behaviour.
- Display a clear message when an AI service error occurs.

## 5. Responsible AI Governance

The assignment considered the four stages of responsible AI:

| Stage | Intended Approach |
|---|---|
| Govern | Define ownership, review responsibilities and product safeguards. |
| Map | Identify risks involving inaccurate information, budget mismatch and user trust. |
| Measure | Evaluate outputs using a structured test dataset and quality criteria. |
| Manage | Review issues and improve the product based on evaluation findings. |

Human review was used during the prototype evaluation. Automated bias detection and automatic system-pause mechanisms were not implemented.

## 6. Limitations and Future Opportunities

The following areas require further work:

- Validate the growth loop with real users.
- Conduct willingness-to-pay research before finalising subscription prices.
- Measure actual funnel conversion using timestamped analytics.
- Improve full-trip budget adherence.
- Strengthen verification of travel information.
- Test itinerary quality across all trip days.
- Explore sharing and export functionality.
- Introduce more systematic responsible AI monitoring.

## 7. Product Management Takeaway

The project explores how an AI trip-planning experience can move beyond generic itinerary generation by focusing on personalisation, budget awareness and transparent recommendations.

The next stage would require real-user validation, improved evaluation coverage and stronger verification before considering a production release.
