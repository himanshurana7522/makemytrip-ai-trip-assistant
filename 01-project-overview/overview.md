# MakeMyTrip AI Trip Assistant

## Project Overview

**Product:** Trust-Aware Personalised Trip Assistant

**Domain:** Travel Technology | Artificial Intelligence | Product Management

**Project Type:** Independent AI Product Management Prototype

## Product Vision

To help travellers create personalised and budget-conscious travel itineraries while making AI-generated recommendations more transparent and trustworthy.

## Core Problem

Travellers often need to use multiple platforms to research destinations, compare activities, estimate budgets and organise their trips. Generic recommendations may not reflect individual preferences or travel constraints.

## Proposed Solution

An AI-powered trip-planning assistant that generates personalised itineraries based on destination, travel dates, budget, traveller type and interests.

## Product Focus

- Personalised itinerary generation
- Budget-aware recommendations
- Transparent AI-generated suggestions
- Clear identification of information requiring confirmation
- Saved trip management

## Important Note

This is an independent educational product prototype developed as part of an AI Product Management learning project. It is not an official MakeMyTrip product.
